# Scrum Master — Certified Knowledge

Based on: Scrum Guide 2020 (Schwaber & Sutherland) + Certiprof SPOPC & SDPC

## Quick Reference

### The Scrum Team (3 Roles)
- **Product Owner**: Maximizes product value. Owns Product Backlog, defines Product Goal
- **Scrum Master**: Serves the team and organization. Removes impediments, teaches Scrum
- **Developers**: Cross-functional professionals delivering a usable Increment each Sprint

### Events
- **Sprint**: Container for all other events. Fixed length ≤ 1 month. New Sprint starts immediately after previous
- **Sprint Planning**: Max 8h (month Sprint). Defines Sprint Goal + selected PBIs + plan
- **Daily Scrum**: 15 min. Developers inspect progress toward Sprint Goal
- **Sprint Review**: Max 4h. Inspect Increment + adapt Product Backlog with stakeholders
- **Sprint Retrospective**: Max 3h. Inspect team's process and create improvement plan

### Artifacts + Commitments
- **Product Backlog** → Product Goal (long-term objective)
- **Sprint Backlog** → Sprint Goal (single objective for the Sprint)
- **Increment** → Definition of Done (quality standard)

### Key Principles
- **Empiricism**: Knowledge comes from experience. Decisions based on observation
- **3 Pillars**: Transparency, Inspection, Adaptation
- **Scrum Values**: Commitment, Focus, Openness, Respect, Courage

### Typical Sprint Pattern
```
Sprint Planning (Wed) → Daily Scrums (Mon-Fri) → Sprint ends (Tue)
  → Sprint Review + Retrospective (Tue) → Next Planning (Wed)
```

### Product Owner Specific (SPOPC)
- Owns Product Backlog content, ordering, and clarity
- Represents stakeholders and business needs
- Defines acceptance criteria for PBIs
- Sprints are NOT a contract — scope can be renegotiated during Sprint
- Stakeholder management: keep them engaged, manage expectations

### Scrum Developer Specific (SDPC)
- Self-managing, cross-functional team (no sub-teams or hierarchies)
- Owns the Sprint Backlog
- Holds each other accountable as professionals
- 3-9 developers typical. Together they create the Definition of Done
- Quality is non-negotiable — DoD must be met for every PBI
